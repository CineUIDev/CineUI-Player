## Free. No Ads. No Subscriptions.

A cinematic video player for macOS, originally built for myself.

What started as a late-night experiment slowly turned into something much bigger than I expected — so I decided to share it.

CineUI is designed around one simple idea:

> The player should disappear when the movie begins.

## ✨ CineUI

- 4K & HDR playback
- MKV, MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, AVI, TS, MTS, M2TS, MPEG, MPG
- Scene Preview while scrubbing
- Audio & subtitle controls
- Multiple interface languages
- Light & Dark experiences
- Experimental visual worlds including Millennium, Pixellennium and Noarium

CineUI is currently in **Alpha** and is actively evolving.

Your feedback matters. If something feels wrong, unnecessary, missing — or you simply have an idea — please open an Issue.

## Open-Core

CineUI Player follows an **open-core** model.

Selected components and developer-facing parts of the project may be open source, while the core playback technology and proprietary CineUI components remain closed.
