# CineUI Player

### Less player. More movie.

**Free • No Ads • No Subscriptions**

**Current version: 0.6 Alpha Universe**

CineUI Player is a cinematic video player for macOS.

I originally started building it for myself.

What began as a late-night experiment slowly turned into something much bigger than I expected — so I decided to share it.

CineUI is built around one simple idea:

> The player should disappear when the movie begins.

## ✨ What is CineUI?

CineUI tries to make the player feel like part of the movie experience rather than another utility window sitting on top of it.

The interface stays quiet when you watch, but becomes expressive when you interact with it.

### Current features

- 4K & HDR playback
- MKV, MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, AVI, TS, MTS, M2TS, MPEG and MPG
- Scene Preview while scrubbing
- Audio and subtitle controls
- Multiple interface languages
- Light and Dark experiences
- Experimental visual worlds including **Millennium**, **Pixellennium** and **Noarium**
- No advertising
- No subscriptions

## 🌌 0.6 Alpha Universe

**Universe** is the current Alpha generation of CineUI Player.

CineUI is still actively evolving.

Some ideas may change, some features may be rebuilt, and new experiments will appear along the way.

That's part of the project.

If you try CineUI, I would genuinely like to know:

- What feels good?
- What feels unnecessary?
- What is confusing?
- What is missing?
- What would make you choose CineUI as your everyday player?

Feedback, bug reports and ideas are welcome through GitHub Issues.

## 🔓 Open-Core

CineUI Player follows an **open-core** model.

Selected components and developer-facing parts of the project may be open source, while the core playback technology and proprietary CineUI components remain closed.

This allows CineUI to stay extensible while preserving the identity and core technology of the project.
